Our Medical Services

We provide a comprehensive range of specialized medical services with the highest quality standards

Physical Therapy

Comprehensive physical therapy services to restore movement and improve quality of life.

Occupational Therapy

We help patients develop daily living skills and regain independence.

Speech & Communication Therapy

Specialized treatment for communication and swallowing problems with proven results.

Elderly Care

Comprehensive compassionate care tailored to the special needs of the elderly.

Chronic Condition Rehabilitation

Advanced therapeutic programs for chronic conditions supervised by experienced specialists.

Advanced Equipment Sessions

The latest therapeutic technologies to achieve effective and measurable results.

Hand Injuries & Nerve Compression

We assess and treat injuries and conditions of the upper extremities from shoulder to hands.

Women's Health

We provide specialized therapeutic sessions and exercises for women to treat common pelvic problems such as urinary incontinence and chronic pelvic pain.

Delayed Motor & Developmental Skills in Children

We help you through a specialized team in treating and diagnosing developmental delays, sensory disabilities, cerebral palsy cases (hemiplegia, quadriplegia, and brachial plexus injury).

Spine & Back Conditions

We help you prevent and treat spinal problems such as scoliosis and kyphosis and their associated postural and functional disorders.

Balance Disorders & Gait Dysfunction

We help you prevent and treat falls and balance disorders resulting from vestibular system disorders and tinnitus.

Lymphedema Cases

We assess cases of swelling and fluid accumulation in organs and hands resulting from injuries and surgeries, and provide an appropriate treatment plan for lymphatic drainage.
